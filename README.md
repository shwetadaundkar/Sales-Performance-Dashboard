📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance across different regions and products. The dashboard tracks key performance indicators, identifies sales trends, and evaluates profitability to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Monitor overall sales and profitability.
* Compare sales performance across different regions.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Understand profit margins and business performance.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI: Interactive dashboard development and visualization.
* SQL: Data querying and analysis.
* Microsoft Excel: Data preparation and initial analysis.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales: Measures overall sales revenue.
* Total Profit: Tracks the profit generated from sales.
* Profit Margin: Measures profit as a percentage of sales.

📊 Dashboard Features

* Sales by Region: Compares sales performance across geographical regions.
* Top Products: Highlights the best-performing products based on sales.
* Monthly Sales Trend: Tracks sales performance over time.
* Interactive Analysis: Enables users to explore sales performance through dashboard visuals and filters, where available.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

* Regions contributing the most to total sales.
* Products generating the highest sales.
* Monthly sales growth and declining trends.
* Profitability and profit margin patterns.

💼 Business Recommendations

* Focus on regions with opportunities for sales growth.
* Review product performance to optimize sales strategies.
* Investigate months with declining sales.
* Monitor profit margins to support better pricing and profitability decisions.
